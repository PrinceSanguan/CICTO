Testing Guide for the
Document Tracking System

This guide walks you through testing the system before it goes into daily use in your offices. No technical knowledge is needed — just follow the steps.

Prepared for: City Government of Baliwag
System: CICTO Document Tracking System
Stage: User Acceptance Testing (UAT)

1What we are doing here

The system is built, and we have tested it thoroughly ourselves. But the test that matters most is yours — you are the ones who know how paperwork actually moves through Baliwag, and we do not.

This guide takes you through every important part of the system. Whenever something looks wrong, behaves oddly, or simply is not clear, please write it down. Section 9 explains how to report it so we can act on it quickly.

Important: You cannot break anything. This is a test system holding sample data, not real city records. Click freely, submit freely, and make mistakes freely — that is what it is for.

How long will this take?

Roughly 60 to 90 minutes to cover everything. You are welcome to split it across several days. It works better with two or three people testing rather than one — different people notice different things.

3Testing as Staff (User)

Log in first as clerk@cicto.test.

3.1 — Logging in

  1. Enter the email and password, then press Login.
  2. Try a wrong password too, so you can see the message.
What should happen: With the right password you land on Home. With the wrong one, a red message appears under the Email field — the screen should not error out or go blank.

3.2 — Registering a new document

  1. Press Track Documents, then Submit Document.
  2. Fill in the Title, Document Type, Department and Priority.
  3. Deliberately attach no file, then press Submit.
  4. Now attach any PDF or image and press Submit again.
What should happen: Step 3 is refused — a file is required. Step 4 shows a green message with a new control number (for example MO-2026-00019). Please write that number down.
A question for you: Is it right that a file is always required? Sometimes only paper arrives at the counter and nothing has been scanned yet. If you would rather allow registration without a file, tell us and we will change it quickly.

3.3 — Finding a document

  1. Go to Track Documents.
  2. Type a control number into the search box and press Search.
  3. Try lower case as well — for example mo-2026-00001.
  4. Use the Status dropdown to filter the list.
  5. Press View on any document.
What should happen: The document is found regardless of capitalisation. In View you can see which office holds it now, how long it has been there, and every office it has passed through.

3.4 — QR codes

  1. Inside a document, press Show QR or Print label.
  2. Check that it is legible and fits the sticker size you intend to use.
What should happen: A QR code appears with the control number printed beneath it, ready to print.

4Testing as an Admin

Log out, then log in as admin@cicto.test.

4.1 — The document workflow (the most important test)

  1. Open the document you registered earlier.
  2. Press Received, then Confirm.
  3. Press Approved, then Confirm.
  4. Press Completed, then Confirm.
What should happen: Each step shows a green message and advances the bar at the top: Initiated → Under Review → Approved → Completed. The history below records who did what, and when.

4.2 — Forwarding to another office

  1. On a different document, press Send to Another Office.
  2. Choose an office, add remarks, and Confirm.
  3. Log out and log in as the receiving office (for example mto@cicto.test).
What should happen: The receiving office now sees the document in their list.

4.3 — Offices must not see each other's documents

This is the most important security rule in the system: one office must never see another office's paperwork.

  1. As admin@cicto.test, search for SB-2026-00001.
  2. Log out, then log in as sb@cicto.test.
  3. Look at what they can see under Track Documents.
What should happen: Step 1 returns nothing at all. In step 3, Sangguniang Bayan sees exactly one document — their own — and nothing from the Mayor's Office.
If you can see another office's documents, that is a serious defect. Please tell us immediately.

4.4 — The Admin Panel

  1. In the sidebar, press Document Management.
  2. Look at the four boxes at the top — are the counts right?
  3. Press Users — your office's staff are listed.
  4. Press Reports — a chart plus a list of what is pending.
  5. Press Settings — change the Date Format, press Save Changes, then reload the page.
What should happen: The setting survives the reload. Under Users, only your own office appears.

4.5 — Reports and exports

  1. In the top menu, press Reports.
  2. Look at the four boxes and the charts.
  3. Press Download PDF, then Export Excel.
  4. Open both downloaded files.
What should happen: Both the PDF and the Excel file open cleanly, and the figures inside are correct.
Please check these carefully. These are the reports you will be passing upward. If a column is missing or you need different figures, now is by far the best time to say so.

5Testing as Super Admin

Log in as super@cicto.test.

5.1 — Creating a new account

  1. In the sidebar, press Manage Users.
  2. Press Add New Admin.
  3. Fill in the name, a new email address, choose the Role and Office, and set a strong password.
  4. Press Create account.
  5. Log out and try logging in with the new account.
What should happen: The new account appears in the list and can log in straight away.
Note: Because email is not yet configured (see section 7), no email is sent to the new member of staff. You will need to give them the password yourself.

5.2 — All Documents

  1. Press All Documents.
  2. Check that you can see documents from every office.
  3. Try the search box and the status filter.
What should happen: Only the Super Admin sees everything. Each row offers View and Archive.
Why is there no “Delete”? This is deliberate. A registered document should never be destroyed — its entire history goes with it, and preserving that history is the whole reason for having this system. Archive does the job instead: the document leaves the working lists but the record remains, and it can be restored at any time.

5.3 — Reports & Analytics and System Settings

  1. Press Reports & Analytics — three charts.
  2. Press System Settings.
  3. Lower down, find the Security log — it records every login, download and account change.
  4. Try Backup.
What should happen: The security log shows your own login from earlier, and the account you created in 5.1.

6Testing on a phone

Many of your staff will use this on a phone, so it is worth testing there. Simply open the same address in your phone's browser.

  • Track Documents — each row should become a card rather than a wide table.
  • Submit Document — every field should fit on screen.
  • The top menu should collapse into three lines (☰).
  • You should never need to swipe sideways to read anything.
What should happen: Everything is readable without zooming or scrolling left and right.

7What does not work yet — and why

In the interest of being straightforward: a few things deliberately do not work yet, because they need information or setup from your side. These are not defects. Please do not spend time reporting them.

7.1 — Email is not configured (the big one)

No mail service is connected to the server yet, which means:

  • “Forgot Password” does not work. No email will arrive. For now, the Super Admin resets the password for anyone who forgets.
  • Support tickets are recorded inside the system only and are not emailed to anyone. The system says so plainly when you submit one.
  • There are no email notifications when a new document reaches your office.
What we need from you: the city's SMTP details (server address, username, password, port). Once we have those, everything above starts working — no change to the software is needed.

7.2 — The camera in the QR scanner

Scanning with a device camera only works when the site is served over HTTPS (the secure address with a padlock). That is a rule enforced by web browsers, not something we can change in the software. Once the system is installed on a real server with HTTPS, it will work.

In the meantime, USB barcode scanners work, and so does typing the control number by hand.

7.3 — Office hours on the Contact page

The Contact Support page reads “Monday – Thursday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM”. We copied this exactly from the design you supplied. We suspect it should read Monday – Friday — please confirm and we will correct it immediately.

8What we still need from you

To finish the system properly we need the following. Written answers (email or chat) are best, so that both sides have something to refer back to later.

What we needWhy
The real list of offices and document types What is in the system now is provisional. We need the actual list, including each office's code — that code becomes part of every control number.
Expected turnaround days per document type The “Due soon” and “Overdue” warnings are calculated from these.
SMTP / email details So password resets, notifications and support tickets work (section 7.1).
How long files should be kept Needed for the retention policy. For example: after how many years may an old version of a file be cleared?
Where the system will be hosted So we can arrange HTTPS, automated backups, and where the backup copies are stored off-site.
Who may approve their own submission Can an Admin approve a document they submitted themselves? Do you have a policy on this?

9How to report a problem

When you find something wrong, please write down these four things. It makes an enormous difference to how fast we can find it:

  1. Which page? For example: “On Submit Document.”
  2. Which account were you using? For example: “admin@cicto.test.”
  3. What did you do, step by step?
  4. What did you expect, and what actually happened?
A screenshot helps enormously. On Windows, press Windows + Shift + S. On a phone, the usual screenshot will do.

An example of a good report

“On Submit Document, using clerk@cicto.test: I filled in everything and attached a PDF, then pressed Submit. I expected a control number to appear, but the screen went blank and nothing happened. Screenshot attached.”

Where to send it

Please send your findings to us through the channel we agreed. If you have found several things, a single list is easier than separate messages.

Thank you. The time you spend on this testing is what makes sure the system is right before it ever touches a real city document. Every small thing you notice now is a problem your staff will not have to live with every day.
