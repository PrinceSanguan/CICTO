Part 2. Adding a new document

Log in as clerk@cicto.test.

  1. Click Track Documents at the top.
  2. Click the Submit Document button.
  3. Fill in the Title, Document Type, Department and Priority.
  4. Do not attach a file yet. Click Submit.

It should refuse, and tell you a file is needed.

  1. Now attach any file from your computer. A PDF or a photo will do.
  2. Click Submit again.

You should see a green message with a new number, something like MO-2026-00019. Write that number down, you will need it later.

Part 3. Finding a document

  1. Click Track Documents.
  2. Type the number you wrote down into the search box. Click Search.
  3. Try it again, but type it in small letters this time.

It should find the document both times. Big or small letters should not matter.

  1. Use the Status drop-down to show only some documents.
  2. Click View on any document.

You should see which office has the document now, how long it has been there, and every office it has passed through.

  1. While looking at a document, click Show QR or Print label.

A QR square should appear with the document number printed under it. Please check it looks clear enough to stick on a folder.

Anything wrong? Write it here:

Part 4. Moving a document along

This is the most important part. Log out, then log in as admin@cicto.test.

  1. Open the document you made in Part 2.
  2. Click Received, then click Confirm.
  3. Click Approved, then click Confirm.
  4. Click Completed, then click Confirm.

After each click you should see a green message, and the bar at the top of the page should move forward one step. At the bottom you should see a list of who did what, and when.

Sending it to another office

  1. Open a different document.
  2. Click Send to Another Office.
  3. Choose an office, type a short remark, and confirm.
  4. Log out. Log in as mto@cicto.test.

The Treasurer's Office should now see that document in their list.

Why is there no Delete button? This is on purpose. Once a document is in the system it should never be wiped out, because its whole history would go with it, and keeping that history is the main reason for having this system at all. Instead there is Archive, which puts a document away but keeps the record, and you can bring it back any time.

Things that do not work yet

So you do not waste time on them, here is what we already know is not finished. These are waiting on information from your side. Please do not report these.

1. The system cannot send email yet

Because of this:

  • "Forgot Password" does nothing. No email will arrive. For now, the system manager sets a new password for anyone who forgets theirs.
  • Help messages are saved but not sent. If someone uses the Submit a Ticket page, the message is kept inside the system. Nobody gets an email about it. The page says so when you use it.
  • Nobody is emailed when a new document arrives at their office.

To switch this on we need your email settings from whoever looks after the city's email. Once we have them, all three work straight away and nothing else has to change.

2. Scanning with a phone camera

Phone cameras will only scan when the website address is the secure kind (the one with a padlock next to it). That is a rule made by the internet browsers themselves, not something we can switch on. Once the system is installed properly on the city's own address, it will work.

A plug-in barcode scanner works now, and so does typing the number by hand.

3. Office hours on the Contact page

The Contact page says Monday to Thursday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. We copied that exactly from the design you gave us. We think it should say Monday to Friday. Please confirm and we will fix it.

If something goes wrong, tell us this

The more of these you can tell us, the faster we can fix it. Even two or three is a big help.

  1. Which page were you on? For example, "the Submit Document page".
  2. Which log-in were you using? For example, "admin@cicto.test".
  3. What did you click, in order?
  4. What did you expect, and what happened instead?

A picture of the screen helps a lot. On a computer, hold down the Windows key and Shift and press S. On a phone, just take a normal screenshot.

An example

"On the Submit Document page, logged in as clerk@cicto.test. I filled in everything and attached a PDF, then clicked Submit. I expected to get a document number, but the screen just went white and nothing happened. Picture attached."

Send your notes to us the same way we normally talk. If you found several things, one list is easier for everyone than lots of separate messages.

Thank you for doing this. The time you spend now is what makes sure the system is right before it touches real city paperwork. Anything you notice today is something your staff will not have to put up with every day.
