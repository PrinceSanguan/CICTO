Part 2. Adding a new document

Log in as clerk@cicto.test.

  1. Click Track Documents at the top.
  2. Click the Submit Document button.
  3. Fill in the Title, Document Type, Department and Priority.
  4. Do not attach a file yet. Click Submit.

It should refuse, and tell you a file is needed.

  1. Now attach any file from your computer. A PDF or a photo will do.
  2. Click Submit again.

You should see a green message with a new number, something like OCM-2026-00007. Write that number down, you will need it later.

Part 3. Finding a document

  1. Click Track Documents.
  2. Type the number you wrote down into the search box. Click Search.
  3. Try it again, but type it in small letters this time.

It should find the document both times. Big or small letters should not matter.

  1. Use the Status drop-down to show only some documents.
  2. Click View on any document.

You should see which office has the document now, how long it has been there, and every office it has passed through.

  1. While looking at a document, click Show QR or Print label.

A QR square should appear with the document number printed under it. Please check it looks clear enough to stick on a folder.

Part 4. Moving a document along

This is the most important part. Log out, then log in as admin@cicto.test.

  1. Open the document you made in Part 2.
  2. Click Received, then click Confirm.
  3. Click Completed, then click Confirm.

There is no Approved button any more, as you asked on 3 September. An office presses Received and that is all it has to do — if the document was sent to several offices, receiving it passes it straight to the next office on the list, and the last office receiving it completes the document by itself.

After each click you should see a green message, and the bar at the top of the page should move forward one step. At the bottom you should see a list of who did what, and when.

Sending it to another office

  1. Open a different document.
  2. Click Send to Another Office.
  3. Choose Office of the City Treasurer, type a short remark, and confirm. The list offers 51 offices. Only two of them have a practice account, and this is one — so please pick this one, or the next step will show you nothing.
  4. Log out. Log in as mto@cicto.test.

The Office of the City Treasurer should now see that document in their list.

Log in as super@cicto.test.

  1. On the left, click Manage Users.
  2. Click Add New Admin.
  3. Fill in a name and an email address, choose the role and office, and make up a long password. Please use a real address you can open — Part 10 needs one.

  3. Read what the panel says will happen. Type your own password in the box that asks for it, then a new password for them, twice.
  4. Click Set password.
  5. Log out and log in as that account using the new password.
  6. Log out again and try the old password.

The new password works. The old one does not. The message at the top reminds you to give them the password yourself. The system never emails anybody a password, even now that it can send email.

  1. Log back in as super@cicto.test and return to Manage Users.
  2. Look at your own row in the list.

There is no Set password button on your own row. That is on purpose — you change your own password under Settings, where it asks for your current one first.

  1. Still as super@cicto.test, click All Documents.
  2. Check that you can see documents from every office here.
  3. Click Reports & Analytics to see the charts.
  4. Click System Settings, then scroll down to the Security log.

The security log should show your own log-in from earlier, the new account you just made, and the password you set for it — that last line names you, the account it was for, and how many devices were signed out. Every password an administrator sets is recorded this way.

Why is there no Delete button? This is on purpose. Once a document is in the system it should never be wiped out, because its whole history would go with it, and keeping that history is the main reason for having this system at all. Instead there is Archive, which puts a document away but keeps the record, and you can bring it back any time.

Part 10. Getting back in when a password is forgotten

This part is new. Until 23 August the system could not send any email, and an earlier version of this sheet said so. It can now, and we would like this tested properly — it is the thing your staff will need on the morning somebody cannot get in.

You need an account whose inbox you can actually open. The five practice log-ins end in @cicto.test, which is not a real address, so use the account you made in Part 8 with your own work address.

  1. Log out. On the Login page, click Forgot Password.
  2. Type that email address and click Email password reset link. It can take a second or two.
  3. Open your inbox. If nothing has arrived after a minute, look in the junk or spam folder.
  4. Click the link in the message. Type a new password twice and save.
  5. Log in with the new password.
  6. Go back to the message and click the same link a second time.

The message arrives from the CICTO office Gmail account. The link takes you to a page where you choose the new password — nobody is ever emailed a password itself. The second click should be refused: a link works once, and stops working an hour after it was sent. Asking for another link straight away is also held back for about a minute. All three of those are on purpose.

If no message ever arrives, please tell us. That is a fault now, not a known limitation. Check the junk folder first, and check on Manage Users that the address on the account is spelled correctly.

Somebody who cannot receive email at all is still covered. An administrator sets their password directly from Manage Users, which you tested in Part 8. That has not gone away, and it is still the right answer for a staff member with no working inbox.

One more thing, if you have five minutes. A person who makes their own account from the Register link now has to confirm their email address before the account can be used.

  1. Log out, click Register, and sign up with an address you can open.
  2. Before opening your inbox, try to reach Track Documents.
  3. Now open the message that arrived and click the link in it.

Before you click the link you should be held on a page asking you to confirm the address, and let no further into the system. After you click it you should be let straight in. An account that has signed itself up also has no office yet, so it cannot put a document in until an administrator gives it one. That is on purpose too.

1. The system does send email — this changed on 23 August

You told us on 20 August that CICTO cannot give out email settings for this system, and we built the alternative you asked for instead. That answer still stands, that alternative is still there, and nothing is waiting on you. What changed is that on 23 August 2026 the system was given an email account of its own, and it was tested with a real message that really arrived. So the things on this page that used to be about missing email are now things to try rather than things to skip:

  • "Forgot Password" works. The page asks for an email address again and sends a link that lets the person set their own new password. Please test it — Part 10 takes you through it step by step. If a link does not arrive, that is a fault and we want to hear about it.
  • A person who signs themselves up must confirm their address first. Anyone using the Register link is sent a confirmation message and cannot use the system until they click the link in it. Accounts you make on Manage Users are not affected: you typed the address, so those can sign in at once.
  • You can still set a password yourself, and sometimes you should. Go to Manage Users, find the person, and press Set password. You will be asked for your own password first, then theirs twice. This is the answer for a staff member with no email address, or who cannot get at their inbox. Tell them the new password in person or over the phone — the system never emails a password to anybody, then or now.
  • Help messages are now sent as well as saved. If someone uses the Submit a Ticket page, the message is still kept inside the system, and it is now also emailed to the support address. If that send fails, the page tells you plainly that the ticket was recorded but not delivered, and gives you the phone number instead.
  • Nobody is emailed when a new document arrives at their office. That has not changed, and we do not plan to change it. The bell inside the system still tells them.

The account it sends from is an ordinary free mailbox, which is allowed roughly five hundred messages a day — far beyond anything this system will do. If you would rather it sent from an official baliwag.gov.ph address later on, tell us and we will move it across. Nothing here has to be undone first.

1a. Two things to know before you set someone's password

  • They get signed out everywhere. If they are signed in on a phone and a desk computer, both stop working straight away. Warn them, or they will ring you about it.
  • There is a tick box for a stolen account. If somebody has simply forgotten their password, leave it alone. Tick it only if you think another person has got into their account, or if they have lost the phone they use for their security codes. It removes those extra security methods so they can set them up again from scratch.

2. Scanning with a phone camera

Phone cameras will only scan when the website address is the secure kind (the one with a padlock next to it). That is a rule made by the internet browsers themselves, not something we can switch on. Once the system is installed properly on the city's own address, it will work.

A plug-in barcode scanner works now, and so does typing the number by hand.

This one is settled. The Contact page now says Monday to Thursday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM, which is what you confirmed on 18 August 2026. There is nothing left for you to decide here.

It used to say 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM, copied exactly from the design you gave us, and we had guessed that the Thursday was a slip of the pen for Friday. It was not. The four-day week is real; only the times were wrong. Please still open the Contact page and check that it reads that way, so we know the screen matches the answer you sent.

If something goes wrong, tell us this

The more of these you can tell us, the faster we can fix it. Even two or three is a big help.

  1. Which page were you on? For example, "the Submit Document page".
  2. Which log-in were you using? For example, "admin@cicto.test".
  3. What did you click, in order?
  4. What did you expect, and what happened instead?

A picture of the screen helps a lot. On a computer, hold down the Windows key and Shift and press S. On a phone, just take a normal screenshot.

An example

"On the Submit Document page, logged in as clerk@cicto.test. I filled in everything and attached a PDF, then clicked Submit. I expected to get a document number, but the screen just went white and nothing happened. Picture attached."

Send your notes to us the same way we normally talk. If you found several things, one list is easier for everyone than lots of separate messages.
